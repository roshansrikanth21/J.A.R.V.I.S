Write a professional technical report with the following structure:

# [TITLE]

## Executive Summary
A 2-3 sentence high-level summary for non-technical stakeholders.

## Background
Context and motivation for this work.

## Methodology
Step-by-step process used. Tools, frameworks, and approach.

## Findings
Detailed results. Use tables and bullet points where appropriate.

## Analysis
What the findings mean. Patterns, anomalies, insights.

## Recommendations
Actionable next steps, prioritized by impact.

## Conclusion
One-paragraph closing summary reinforcing the key takeaway.

---
Tone: Professional, precise, confident. Avoid jargon unless necessary.
Format: Markdown. Use headings, tables, code blocks where appropriate.
Length: As detailed as the subject demands — do not pad or truncate.
