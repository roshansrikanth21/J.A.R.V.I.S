Write a professional formal email with:

- Clear subject line
- Appropriate salutation
- Context paragraph (why you're writing)
- Main body (the actual request / information)
- Call to action (what you need from them)
- Professional closing

Tone: Formal but not stiff. Respectful, direct, concise.
Length: Under 200 words unless the content demands more.
Do not use filler phrases like "I hope this email finds you well."
